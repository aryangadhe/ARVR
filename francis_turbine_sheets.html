RULE · EULER TURBINE LAW
Euler head is Hₑ = (Vw₁·u₁ - Vw₂·u₂) / g. For optimum efficiency, water leaves radially (Vw₂ = 0), yielding maximum energy conversion: Hₑ = Vw₁·u₁ / g.

How does gate opening govern power?
Hold turbine speed N constant at rated rpm by adjusting brake load as guide vane opening varies from 20% to 100%.

CARD CSCALING · SIMILITUDE
Unit speed, unit flow, unit power
Collapse multi-head laboratory test runs onto unified dimensionless curves to predict performance under any reservoir head.
  • Calculate Unit Speed Nu = N / √H, Flow Qu = Q / √H, and Power Pu = P / H^(3/2).
  • Superimpose readings taken at 15 m, 20 m, and 25 m heads on one chart.
  • A unified master envelope validates dynamic similarity and blade similitude.
  • Standard industrial method for scaling scale-model data to full-size dams.

CARD DMOMENTUM · EULER EQUATION
The energy exchange in the runner
Angular momentum flux entering at the runner rim must equal the shaft torque produced plus exit swirl.
  • Measure inlet whirl Vw₁ = V₁ cos α₁ and calculate runner peripheral speed u₁.
  • Calculate theoretical Euler head Hₑ = (Vw₁ · u₁ - Vw₂ · u₂) / g.
  • Compare Euler head Hₑ with net manometric head H from gauges.
  • The gap (H - Hₑ) quantifies hydraulic friction and blade passage loss.

CARD EDIFFUSER · CAVITATION
Why the draft tube saves energy
A divergent draft tube decelerates discharge flow, converting exit kinetic head back into suction pressure below the runner.
  • Recovers exit kinetic energy: ΔHrec = ηdt · (V₂² - V₃²) / 2g.
  • Allows placing turbine above tail water by height Hs with zero head penalty.
  • Calculate Thoma's cavitation factor: σ = (Hb - Hs - hva) / H.
  • Prevents vapor pocket formation that causes blade pitting and destructive noise.

C1ENGINEERING CONTEXT
Why Francis turbines?
Over 60% of world hydropower is generated by Francis turbines. Here is why the reaction principle dominates global clean energy.
⚡ Baseload & grid stability
Instant black-start and fast spinning inertia balance transient grid fluctuations.
🔋 Pumped storage (PHS)
Reversible pump-turbines act as massive water batteries for solar/wind surplus.
🌊 Medium head mastery
Operates efficiently across 30 m to 600 m heads, unmatched by Pelton or Kaplan.
📈 Peak 95%+ efficiency
Computerized CFD runner vane geometry captures virtually all fluid momentum.
🏞️ Multi-purpose dams
Integrates seamlessly into municipal flood control and irrigation networks.
🎓 Teach the physics
Laboratory test rig demonstrates angular momentum and Euler reaction forces.

SECTION E · EXPERIMENT & CALCULATIONS
Run · Regulate · Compute
Two panels: how to run it, and how to work it out. Fill in the brackets from your own apparatus and verify turbine overall efficiency.
THE EXPERIMENT STEP BY STEP
Run it in six steps
01
Check & Prime · Ensure adequate water in sump, prime centrifugal pump, open brake cooling water, verify zero load on hanger.
02
Start Pump · Switch on pump motor with delivery valve closed; slowly open delivery throttle to pressurise spiral casing.
03
Set Gate Opening · Adjust wicket gate regulating handwheel to designated opening (e.g. 100%, 75%, 50% gate).
04
Apply Brake Load · Hang dead weights W on rope hanger; note spring reading S and steady speed N with tachometer.
05
Record Gauges · Note casing pressure Pi, draft tube vacuum Pv, and mercury manometer deflection Δh across Venturimeter.
06
Vary Load & Repeat · Increment weights in uniform steps up to maximum load; repeat readings, then unload drum before stopping pump.
SAFETY: Ensure continuous cooling water to brake drum during all loaded runs. Never abruptly shut the delivery valve or supply pump motor under heavy load. Verify all electrical earthing before starting.

TURBINE RIG CONSTANTS
Brake drum dia D = 0.28 m, rope d = 0.02 m
Effective radius Reff = (D+d)/2 = 0.15 m
Venturimeter d₁ = 100 mm, d₂ = 60 mm, Cd = 0.98
NET TOTAL HEAD (H)
Pi = 1.60 kg/cm² = 16.00 m of water
Pv = 150 mm Hg = 0.150 × 13.6 = 2.04 m
Datum z = 0.35 m → H = 18.39 m
DISCHARGE (Q) VIA VENTURIMETER
Manometer Δhm = 12.5 cm Hg = 0.125 m Hg
Head h = 0.125 × (13.6 - 1) = 1.575 m of water
Q = 0.0224 m³/s = 22.4 L/s
OUTPUT SHAFT POWER (Pₒ)
Load: W = 12.0 kg, S = 2.2 kg, N = 950 rpm
Torque T = (12.0 - 2.2) × 9.81 × 0.15 = 14.42 N·m
Pₒ = 2π × 950 × 14.42 / 60000 = 1.434 kW
WATER INPUT POWER (Pin)
Pin = (ρ · g · Q · H) / 1000
Pin = (1000 × 9.81 × 0.0224 × 18.39) / 1000
Pin = 4.041 kW
OVERALL EFFICIENCY (ηₒ)
ηₒ = (Pₒ / Pin) × 100%
ηₒ = (1.434 / 4.041) × 100%
ηₒ = 35.49%
